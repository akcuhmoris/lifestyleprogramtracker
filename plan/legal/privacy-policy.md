## 2. What we collect

We try to collect the minimum amount of data needed to make the Service work.

### Information you provide directly

- **Account information.** Email address, password (hashed via our auth provider), and — if you sign in with Google or Apple — your name and profile picture if you choose to share them.
- **Profile information.** Optional display name and timezone.
- **Program data.** Your daily task completions, journal entries, weight logs, notes, progress photos, and program settings (length, task list, etc.).

### Information we collect automatically

- **Device and connection.** IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, app version, and similar technical identifiers used to deliver the Service and detect abuse.
- **Usage events.** Basic interactions (sign-in, screen views, error events) used to debug and improve the Service. You can opt out of analytics in Settings.
- **Crash diagnostics.** When the app encounters an error, we collect a stack trace and a request ID — never your weight, journal, or photo content.

### Information we do NOT collect

- We do not access your contacts, calendar, location, microphone, or background activity.
- We do not collect biometric identifiers.
- We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
- We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

## 3. How we use your information

- **To run the Service.** Create your account, store your data, sync between your devices, deliver requested features.
- **To improve the Service.** Debug bugs, plan features, prioritize fixes — using aggregated and pseudonymous data.
- **To communicate with you.** Send transactional emails (account verification, password reset, billing receipts if applicable). We do not send marketing emails unless you opt in.
- **To protect the Service.** Detect abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
- **To comply with the law.** Respond to valid legal requests where required.

We do **not** use your data to train AI models.

## 5. How long we keep your data

- **Active accounts:** for as long as you maintain the account.
- **Deleted accounts:** when you delete your account from Settings → Account → Delete account, we mark the account for deletion and send you a confirmation email. The account and all associated data are permanently deleted within **30 days** unless you cancel.
- **Backups:** standard database backups are kept for up to **90 days** after which deleted data is purged from backups too.
- **Logs:** server logs are kept for up to **30 days**; crash reports for up to **90 days**.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data:

- All connections are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- All data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers.
- Authentication uses tokens with short lifetimes and rotation on suspicious activity.
- Database row-level security ensures users can only access their own data.
- Service-role credentials are stored only on the server.
- We rotate sensitive credentials periodically.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach that affects your information, we will notify you within **72 hours** as required by applicable law.
